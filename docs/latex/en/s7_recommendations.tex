\section{Recommendations and implementation}\label{sec-reco}

\begin{objectif}
Turn the results into control rules, and set out the data needed and the monthly procedure.
\end{objectif}

\subsection{What we recommend}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Control the forward, not the split.} For each book, compute the forward line (Totem forwards, FO
dividends) and the non-forward line $\sprod{\eta}{\Delta\ell}$. For forward-linear books, the forward line \emph{is}
the control, and it does not depend on the dividend contributors (\cref{thm-control,thm-exact}). The Totem dividends
are needed only where $\eta\ne0$: dividend swaps and futures, stock borrow and lending, total return swap funding,
American exercise, exotics.
\item \textbf{Do not add up without offsetting} large dividend and repo lines of opposite signs: this is a split
artefact. In prudent valuation, take the forward as the parameter where $\eta\approx0$.
\item \textbf{Judge the curve in carry, against a noise band.} Replace visual checks and thresholds in basis points
of repo by the carry difference $\delta$ and the covariance $\Gamma$ computed from Totem's dispersions and contributor
counts (\cref{prop-noise}).
\item \textbf{Deploy the structural detector} with $\alpha=\pct{2}$, and publish for each alarm the explanation in
natural units, the repaired curve and, separately, the FO mismark test.
\item \textbf{Keep B-splines, mixtures and neural networks downstream}: on the detector's outputs and residuals, and to
pool the calibration by region and category.
\item \textbf{Calibrate and validate on real history}: $\kappa$ and the level scale by region and category, every
quarter, then a validation on twelve to twenty-four months of snapshots with labelled incidents.
\end{enumerate}

If Totem does not publish the dispersion of the contributors' forwards, it can be estimated from the dispersion of deep
in-the-money calls and puts, or calibrated on history: the detector needs the right shape across maturities, and
$\kappa$ corrects a common bias.

\subsection{Monthly procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Load the Totem and FO snapshots at the same valuation time; check the reference spot.
\item Compute the forwards by parity; review the implied discount factor, the parity residuals and the dispersion
ratios.
\item Allocate the Totem dividend levels on the FO ex-date profile; compute the implied repo with the Totem dividends
($q^{\tot}$) and with the FO dividends ($\hat q$).
\item Build $\Gamma$ and run the detector with the $\kappa$ and level scale of the region and category.
\item Compute the control lines per book (forward, dividend, repo, non-forward) and the materiality of the split.
\item Review the alarms with their explanations, and the possible FO mismarks; use the repaired curve when a Totem
anomaly is confirmed.
\item Record the decision taken on each alarm: these labels feed the next calibration and the validation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Governance and next steps}
$\kappa$ and the level scale are recalibrated every quarter by region and category on clean history, with an alarm
budget of \vFPR{} of names per month. The realised alarm rate and the share of explanations accepted by the
controllers are tracked. Where they exist, market anchors complement the detector: listed index and dividend futures,
total return futures, stock borrow fees for the short end. The next step is to run the pipeline on twelve to
twenty-four months of real Totem and FO snapshots, calibrate the settings by region and category, and measure the
quality of the explanations on labelled incidents.

\section*{Conclusion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Conclusion}
Implied repo curves are irregular because they are the residual of two consensus averages computed on different
panels, and part of that residual cannot be removed by any method that only sees the consensus (\cref{thm-nonid}). Two
practical consequences follow. For control, a forward-linear book does not need the split between repo and dividends:
the repo control is the forward control minus the dividend control, exactly under full revaluation, and the
non-forward exposure $\eta$ tells, book by book, when the split matters. For detection, the mechanisms that cause real
anomalies have known shapes in the carry difference; matched filters, with a noise computed from Totem's own
statistics, detect them at a fixed false alarm rate, explain them in natural units and tell them apart from FO
mismarks.
