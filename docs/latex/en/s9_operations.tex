full revaluation, the panel-gap and drop-off formulas, null calibration of the detector and recovery of each template.

\subsection{Monthly procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Load the Totem and FO snapshots at the same valuation time; check the reference spot.
\item Imply forwards by parity; review the implied discount factor, the parity residuals and the dispersion ratios.
\item Allocate the Totem dividend periods on the FO ex-date profile; imply repo with Totem dividends and with FO dividends.
\item Build $\Sigma$ and run the detector with the $\kappa$ and level scale of the region and category.
\item Compute the control lines per book: forward, dividend, non-forward and repo lines; check split materiality.
\item Review alarms with their explanations and FO-mismark candidates; use the repaired curve where a Totem anomaly is confirmed.
\item Record the decision for each alarm; the labels feed the next calibration.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Calibration and governance}
$\kappa$ and the level scale are recalibrated quarterly per region and category on clean history, with an alarm budget
of \vFPR{} of names per month. The realised alarm rate and the share of explanations accepted by reviewers are
monitored. Where they trade, exchange anchors complement the detector: listed index and dividend futures, total-return
futures that quote a financing spread, and securities-lending fees for the short end of single stocks.

\section{Conclusion}
Implied repo curves are irregular because they are the residual of two consensus means computed on different panels;
part of that residual cannot be removed by any method that sees only consensus data (\cref{thm-nonid}). Two
consequences follow. For forward-linear exposure the total control does not need the split, exactly on full revaluation
and to first order on control lines (\cref{thm-control,thm-exact}), and the non-forward exposure $g$ tells, book by book,
how much the split matters. For detection, the mechanisms that do produce bad curves have known signatures in carry space;
matched filters with a noise model built from Totem's own statistics detect them at a calibrated false-alarm rate,
explain them in natural units and keep genuine FO mismarks apart. The next step is to run the pipeline on twelve to
twenty-four months of real Totem and FO snapshots, calibrate the settings per region and category, and measure
attribution against labelled incidents.
