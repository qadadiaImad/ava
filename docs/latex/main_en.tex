\normalsize A technical note with proofs, worked examples and a synthetic benchmark}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent Repo parameters used for independent price verification are implied from Totem: forwards from
consensus call and put prices, dividends from the separate Totem dividend service. Because the two services are
fed by different contributor panels whose sizes change with tenor, the implied repo curve inherits noise and
artefacts from both, and sometimes shows shapes the market does not justify. This note gives (i)~an exact
decomposition of the implied repo into the option panel's repo and a \emph{panel gap}, with the noise, jump and
bias it produces for each mechanism; (ii)~a non-identifiability theorem stating that no method using consensus
data alone can remove that gap; (iii)~a control identity proving that the total of the dividend and repo control
lines does not depend on the dividend panel for forward-linear exposure, together with a materiality measure
computed from sensitivities alone; (iv)~a structural detector built from matched filters and a generalised
likelihood-ratio test, calibrated on clean history, that names the mechanism behind each alarm and repairs the
curve; and (v)~a benchmark on a contributor-level synthetic Totem market comparing it with naive checks and with
B-spline plus Gaussian-mixture clustering. Every statement is proved, illustrated by a worked example and checked
in the accompanying Python package.
\end{abstract}
